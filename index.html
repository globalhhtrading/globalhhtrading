About us

Our name says where we start and where we end. Harvest is the earth: rice, spices, lentils, dry fruits, tea, cotton and plants grown across India. Hide is the craft: leather goods, handcrafted juttis, garments and gifts made by skilled artisans.

We are a registered Indian trading company. We source from trusted farms, mills and manufacturers, check quality before dispatch, and manage packing, documents and shipment, so buyers deal with one partner instead of many. We also supply chemicals, healthcare, construction and industrial products, and source anything else on demand. We are now opening export supply to the Gulf, Africa, Europe and Canada.

  • One supplier, many categories. Food, fashion, eco goods and industrial products under one order.
  • Samples first. Check quality before you commit to bulk.
  • Made to your needs. Custom packing, private labelling and on-demand sourcing.
  • Clear paperwork. Registered business with documentation and shipping support.
